# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, especially in diagnostic reports.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users and the project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of sensitive
data, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using the contact
method in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and as
confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict
participation, or ban contributors when necessary.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
